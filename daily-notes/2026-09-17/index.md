constructible sheaves
view point 1 : "local system with singularities" (on each strata it becomes a local system)
view point 2 : the correct finiteness condition so to Verdier duality holds.

What is Verdier duality? It is a local, generalized version of poincare duality which appears in six functor formalism.


Bousfield localisation : when a fully faithful functor admits a left/right adjoint that can be viewed as a localisation.
some explanation of what is happening : For example projective resolution is a right Bousfield localisation. 
The counit map PX->X gives a good representative (on the left) of X to do calculations for localized cat in the original cat.
right truncation functor is right Bousfield localization.
left truncation functor is left Bousfield localization.


Key idea in perverse sheaves?
Given Z->X<-U one can "Glue t-structures" from D(U), D(Z) to D(X).
Generalizing this, given a stratification, by glueing t-structures (related to local systems) from nonsingular strata, we get
a t-structure on whole D(X) whose heart is "perverse sheaves".
* perverse sheaves seem to be the more correct construction of "local system with singularities" (properly glued)


About geometric representation theory, geometric satake.
By def the cat of perverse sheaves are abelian. It is not monoidal with the usual tensor product but there is a new "convolution" that makes it into a symmetric monoidal category.
Similar to the case of local systems, one can hope this cat to be tannakian and thats the interesting case. (Geometry -> rep theory)
My case of interest is when X=Gr_G, stratification given by cocharacters bounding the position of lattice. The cat of perverse sheaf in this case
is equiv to Rep(G^) dual langland group.
