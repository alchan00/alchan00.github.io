
Trying to understand motives.

algebraic topology allowed "study of shapes and spaces" to be incorporated to "math" by providing rigorous language; algebra.

In the case of algebro-geometric objects/spaces, comming from polynomial equations the study between geometry and algebra is much
more related and fun. In particular there is a Galois action which links number theory and rep theory into the picture.
The shape, geometry, symmetry of varieties blends in to algebraic data leading to interesting open problems such as Hodge and Tate conjecture.

I will restrict my interest to the category C smooth proper varieties over k

The idea of motive is to find the best linear algebraic approximation for C.
The question one wants to answer : We do loose some information we we try to understand C in terms of linear algebra. 
This may look bad but it can be good in some sense that we think of this as throwing away unintersting stuff and only look at what one cares.
What do have left then?

There are some versions of motives including chow motives and numerical motives that tries to achieve this goal.
To check these are "good" definition of ideal motives we need that
1. Any Weil cohomology theory should factor through motives.
2. the category is "linear algebraic" category.

There is some ambiguity in 2, I thinks thats why there are many versions. But ideally I think one should hope for a "tannakian category".
chow motive is not even an abelian category but 1 is always true.
numeric motive can be the answer if standard conjectures are true.
lets denote X->hX denote an object in chow motive.
cohomology theories come as realizations from CHmot(k).


examples
1. Artin motives.
case when X has dim0, which are just seperable field extensions.
tannakian category generated by hX=h^0X is equivalent to Rep_Q(G_k)
this already shows that the field is very important.

2. Abelian motives.
some interesting facts.
Abelian categories up to isogeny embedds fully faithfully to CHMot(k) X->h^1X.
exterior powers of h^1X = h^nX (generates whole hX)
Note that Ab up to isogeny is semisimple abelian but not tannakian. there is no tensor product.
However in CHMot(k) (or num) there is a tannakian cat generated by h^1X called abelian motives Mot^ab(k).
One can also look at tannakian cat generated by single AV A. The algebraic group obtained is called Mumford tate group when k=C, which are automorphisms of H^1(A,Q) somehow preserving hodge structure. general case aSymplectic group. (this seem to he related to Siegel varieties)
