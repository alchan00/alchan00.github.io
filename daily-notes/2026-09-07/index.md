Why study representation theory?
1. Common theme in math is to reduce a hard theory to a well known one; linear algebra.
   ex) homology, calculus, rep theory.

2. In many cases we only encounter the representation of a group in some phenomena ex) galois group acting on etale cohomology
   rather than the group itself.

There is also Tanakian formalism telling that one recovers the group through its representations.
