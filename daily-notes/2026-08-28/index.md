View point on Algebraic invariants.

What I mean by algebraic invariants are for example homology groups attached to a topological space or the dervied category of modules on a site. The formalism that constructs these invariants seem to be complicated and never computable. However, the functoriality of the construction and existences of objects and maps gives powerful machinery. Once you have a machinery "algebraic invariants speak for yourself" in the sense that it endows one with formal and natural language of the description of a geometric phenomena. This viewpoint seem to see through the essence of studying geometry using algebra.
