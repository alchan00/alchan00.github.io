A ring map is formally smooth if it has left lifting property of surjection with nilpotent kernel.
LLP is closed under composition pushout and retract, and one can think of generators :
A->A[X], A[X1,...,X_N]/(f1,...,fN) s.t. jacobian is invertible, A->A_g localisation.
A ring map is smooth iff it is lfp and formally smooth.
